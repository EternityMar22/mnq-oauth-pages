Personal-use workflow

MNQ Backup is an OAuth client for the MNQ owner’s private backup workflow. It is not offered as a hosted backup service to other users, and this website does not accept accounts or backup uploads.

Owner responsibilities

The owner is responsible for configuring Google Drive access, protecting rclone credentials and any Restic passphrase, checking that backup and restore operations succeed, and choosing retention settings. Encryption is provided only when Restic is configured on the backup host.

Availability

This workflow depends on the owner’s server, local configuration, Google Drive, and third-party services. No uninterrupted availability or successful recovery is guaranteed.

Policies

Use of Google Drive and GitHub Pages is also subject to the applicable Google and GitHub terms. See the Privacy Policy for information about data access and revocation.
